Simple Past Tense (Waktu Lampau)

Kapan Kita Menggunakan Simple Past Tense?

Digunakan untuk menceritakan peristiwa, tindakan, atau keadaan yang terjadi dan sudah selesai di masa lampau (completed actions), mendeskripsikan pengalaman liburan, cerita petualangan, atau catatan diari.

1

Was / Were Form (Nominal)

Mendeskripsikan perasaan, kondisi, atau tempat di masa lalu.

Pola Rumus:
Subject + was/were + Adjective / Noun / Place

Gunakan WAS untuk (I, He, She, It, Single noun):

• I was sick yesterday. (Saya dulu sakit)

• She was proud of her score. (Dia bangga)

Gunakan WERE untuk (You, We, They, Plural nouns):

• Joni and Winda were confused.

• They were excited at Karimun Jawa.

2

Verbs Form (Verbal)

Menyatakan tindakan aksi langsung yang dilakukan di masa lalu.

Pola Rumus:
Subject + Past Form (Verb 2) + Object / Adverb
Regular Verbs (+ed / +d):

• play → played: The boys played football.

• visit → visited: The tourist visited Borobudur Temple.

Irregular Verbs (Berubah Bentuk):

• give → gave: Galih gave me some apples.

• see → saw: I saw a Komodo dragon.

• ride → rode: We rode a jeep.
